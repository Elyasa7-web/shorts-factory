# Privacy Policy — Top5 Planet

Top5 Planet is a personal automation that creates and uploads short ranking videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

**What data is accessed.** The app requests a single permission, `youtube.upload`, from the channel owner's Google account so it can upload videos to that channel. It does not read, store, or share any other Google or YouTube data.

**What is stored.** An OAuth refresh token is kept as an encrypted secret in the owner's private GitHub Actions settings. No personal data about viewers or other users is collected.

**Sharing.** No data is sold, shared, or transferred to any third party.

**Revoking access.** Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

**Contact.** Open an issue at https://github.com/Elyasa7-web/shorts-factory/issues.
